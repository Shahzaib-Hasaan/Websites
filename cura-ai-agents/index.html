Is it actually HIPAA-compliant?

Yes — built for healthcare, HIPAA-aligned, and backed by a signed BAA with your practice. Patient data stays secure end to end.

Will it work with our EHR?

Almost certainly. It syncs two-way with Epic, Cerner, athenahealth, NextGen and more — so bookings land exactly where your team already works.

We have a lot of doctors — can it handle that?

That’s exactly what it’s built for. It routes every patient to the right provider, specialty, and location across your whole practice.

How does bringing back old patients work?

It scans for patients you haven’t seen in 6+ months, then calls or texts them (with consent) and books them right back in. Found money from records you already have.

Will patients be able to tell it’s AI?

It sounds natural and on-brand — and the moment anything clinical comes up, it hands off warmly to your staff with the full context.

How fast can we go live?

Days, not months. We train it on your doctors, hours, and rules, connect your systems, and you’re catching calls — no new hardware.
